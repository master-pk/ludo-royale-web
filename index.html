Every piece fights differently

The Rook wears armour and survives the first hit. The Knight strikes the tiles ahead of and behind where it lands. The Pawn is your climber. The King is the only piece that can finish your race.

🃏
Power cards

Every capture earns a card, and bigger victims pay rarer ones. Shield, Snipe, Teleport, Sabotage, Royal Decoy and more. Hold two, play one per turn.

👑
Crown your Pawn

Walk a Pawn home while the throne sits empty and she becomes the Queen. Only one Queen exists in a match. Take her with a Pawn and the crown passes to you.

♚
Destroy the King

Lose your King while your army is still on the board and you are out. Not behind, out. Every roll carries that weight.

About the game

Ludo Royale keeps the board everyone grew up with and changes what stands on it. Your four pieces are chess pieces with their own rules, captures pay out power cards, a brave Pawn can take the crown, and losing your King ends your match on the spot. If you know Ludo, you already know half the rules; a coached first game teaches the rest in about two minutes.

Got an invite from a friend? Open their link. It shows the room code and takes you straight into the match.

No cash prizes. Coins are for play only and can never be withdrawn. Royal Club is an optional monthly subscription that renews until cancelled in your store settings.
