Ludo Royale — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Ludo Royale: Battle of Kings ("the game") is a casual board game published by Factory ("we"). This policy explains what data the game handles and why. The short version: we collect what is needed to run online matches, keep your coins and progress, show ads and sell in-game purchases; we never sell your data, and there are no cash prizes of any kind.

Data we collect

  • Display name and avatar choice — the name you type on your profile is shown to other players in online rooms, match results, friends lists and leaderboards. It is never verified and can be anything you like.
  • Player ID — the game signs you in anonymously (a random identifier issued per install) so online rooms can tell players apart.
  • Optional sign-in with Google or Apple — if you choose to sign in, we store the identifier and email address that Google or Apple provides, in our authentication service (Firebase Authentication) and on your player profile. It is used only to restore your progress on a new device and to match account requests such as deletion; it is never shown to other players and never used for marketing. No password is stored by us.
  • Gameplay data — online matches store the moves of the match (dice rolls, piece moves, cards played, results) on our game server so all players see the same board and games can resume after a disconnect.
  • Progress and wallet — your Royal Coins balance, XP, level, streak, daily-reward history, achievements and unlocked avatars.
  • Social data — your friends list, friend requests, room invites and block list.
  • Purchases — when you buy coins or subscribe to the Royal Club, Google Play or the App Store processes the payment. We receive a purchase token or receipt and the product bought so we can grant it and verify it; we never see your card or bank details.
  • Analytics and crash reports — we use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to understand how the game is used and to fix crashes. This includes app events (for example, "match started"), device model, operating system version, country, app version and a Firebase app instance identifier. This data is not used for advertising.

Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: a "rewarded" ad you may choose to watch for an in-game benefit, and occasional interstitial ads between matches. Royal Club members see no interstitials. To serve these ads, Google's advertising SDK may collect and use an advertising identifier and related device information. Google acts as an independent data controller for this; its use of data is governed by the Google Privacy & Terms. You can reset or limit the advertising identifier in your device settings. We do not use the advertising ID ourselves.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect phone numbers, contacts, precise location, photos or files.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone other than the service providers named here (Google Firebase, Google AdMob, Google Play and Apple), each of which processes it to provide its service.
  • There are no cash prizes: Royal Coins are for play only and can never be withdrawn, transferred or exchanged for money.

Where data lives and for how long

Game data is stored with Google Firebase (servers in the Singapore region) and is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Local games against the computer or pass-n-play never leave your device. Online match logs are purged 30 days after the match ends; abandoned rooms are purged after 24 hours. Your profile, wallet and social data are kept until you delete your account.

Data deletion

You can delete your account and the data tied to it from within the game (open your Profile and tap the bin icon beside the sign-in row) or by following the steps on our account deletion page. That page also lists exactly what is removed and what is retained.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. It requests no personal information beyond an optional display name and an optional Google or Apple sign-in.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated and material changes are announced in the game.
