Ludo Royale — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

Ludo Royale ("the game") is a casual board game published by Factory ("we"). This policy explains what data the game handles and why. The short version: we collect the minimum needed to run online matches, we show no ads, we sell nothing, and we track nothing across other apps or sites.

Data we collect

  • Display name and avatar choice — the name you type on your profile is shown to other players in online rooms and match results. It is never verified and can be anything you like.
  • Anonymous player ID — the game signs in anonymously (a random identifier issued per install) so online rooms can tell players apart. No email, phone number, password, or real identity is requested or stored.
  • Gameplay data — online matches store the moves of the match (dice rolls, piece moves, match results) on our game server so all players see the same board and games can resume after a disconnect.

Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob — a "rewarded" ad you may choose to watch to play an extra game or to revive your King. To serve these ads, Google's advertising SDK may collect and use an advertising identifier and related device information. Google acts as an independent data controller for this; its use of data is governed by the Google Privacy & Terms. We do not receive or store your advertising ID ourselves. There are no in-app purchases.

What we do NOT collect

  • No email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or precise location.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs of our own, and we do not sell your data.
  • Beyond the AdMob advertising identifier described above, we collect no other device identifiers for tracking.

Where data lives

Online-match data is stored with Supabase (our backend provider) and is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Local games against the computer or pass-n-play never leave your device.

Data deletion

Because sign-in is anonymous, uninstalling the game abandons your player ID permanently. If you would like the display name and match history tied to your player ID deleted from our servers, email us (below) from within the game's install period or with your room code / player name and we will remove it within 30 days.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. It requests no personal information beyond an optional display name.
